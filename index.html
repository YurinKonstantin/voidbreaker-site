The game

Void Breaker is a fast single-screen arcade game. You have one ship and the asteroids never stop: every large rock splits into two smaller ones until the sector is clear. From wave three, hunter drones join the fight, and every fifth wave sends a boss into the arena.

Short runs, real pressure

Each attempt lasts 3–8 minutes. Waves arrive faster and denser, asteroids speed up by 4% per wave.

A choice every wave

Clear the arena and pick 1 of 3 upgrades — fire rate, damage, shields, ricochet and eight more.

Combo up to ×8

Uninterrupted kills raise the multiplier; a single hit resets it. Aggressive play scores more and risks more.

Features

  • Single-screen arcade shooter, 3–8 minute runs
  • Endless waves with splitting asteroids, drones and bosses
  • 12 upgrades with a choice of 1 of 3 between waves
  • Combo multiplier up to ×8 for uninterrupted kills
  • Mouse and keyboard controls, dash with invulnerability
  • Local top-10 high score table
  • Options: music and sound volume, language, fullscreen, reduced screen shake
  • Fully offline: no ads, no purchases, no data collection
